About Life

  • Procedural environments. Everyday spaces built from streets, buildings, vegetation, furnished interiors and the objects within them.
  • Illustrative rendering. Ink contours, soft colors and material details give architecture, furniture and household objects a consistent visual style.
  • Changing atmosphere. Daylight, sunset, overcast skies, rain and night reshape the scenes through lighting, reflections, moving traffic and interior illumination.
  • Object construction. Component-based models reveal how everyday products fit together through interactive inspection, separation and reassembly.

About Worlds

  • Procedural planets. Large landforms and fine terrain detail combine to create distinct mountain ridges, cliffs, valleys and coastlines.
  • Ink and terrain materials. Contour lines define the landscape, while elevation and slope shape the distribution of snow, exposed rock, grassland and ochre soil.
  • Fragment-filled space. Animated, reflective glass fragments form distinct orbital patterns and nebula fields around rotating planets.
  • Exploration across scales. Terrain streaming and levels of detail connect planetary views to surface exploration, retaining the original terrain mesh in close views.
